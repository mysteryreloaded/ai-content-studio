# Privacy

What the Studio stores, and what it never does. This is the Operator's
reference; the page your users actually read is the **generated**
privacy page described below.

## The short version

- **No analytics, no ads, no trackers, no third-party scripts.** The web
  app ships no third-party JavaScript, no cookie-consent banner and no
  tracking pixels. The only cookies are one session cookie and the
  short-lived sign-in cookies.
- **Everything user-created lives on your servers.** Uploads, generated
  Assets, prompts and settings are stored on the Studio's own database
  and data volume; user content is sent only to your own ComfyUI
  Backends.
- **The only outside service is Google sign-in**, and only for the
  identity fields below.

## What the Studio stores about a user

- **Identity from Google.** At sign-in the Studio asks Google's OIDC
  endpoint for the account's *id*, *email*, *name* and *picture*. The
  sign-in *tokens* are used to finish the sign-in and are not retained.
  The picture is not copied into the Studio — the UI loads it from
  Google when displayed.
- **Role and status** — `admin` or `user`, and `pending` / `active` /
  `disabled` (a blocked account is a special case of a deleted one; see
  [the consent screen](consent.md)).
- **Accepted terms versions** — which version of the site terms the user
  accepted, and which version of each Model's terms they accepted.
- **User content** — their Uploads, generated Assets, the prompts they
  typed, and their settings.

## Retention

- An **Upload** that no Generation has used is removed 24 hours after
  its last use.
- **Assets** are kept until their owner deletes them, or until
  `ASSET_RETENTION_DAYS` expires, if you set that in your `.env` (blank
  by default, i.e. keep forever).
- **Log entries** (the structured event entries, not the raw server
  log) are deleted after `LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` days — 30 by default.
- **Audit entries** that record an admin's action are kept permanently.

## What the Studio never does

- It does not keep users' **IP addresses** — the only exception is an
  Admin's own address, recorded in the Audit log of that Admin's own
  actions.
- It creates no **public links** to user content: a user's Uploads and
  Assets are visible only to that user and, read-only, to Admins.
- It uses browser **local storage** for nothing but the user's theme
  choice and which Notices they've dismissed — and even those are never
  sent to the server.

## The generated privacy page

Every Studio serves its privacy page at `/privacy`, always linked from
the footer. The page is **generated by the Studio itself from its actual
configuration**, so it can't drift from what the code does:

- it states what Google sign-in is used for — the four identity fields,
  and that the tokens are discarded;
- it states that the data is never sold or shared with anyone;
- it describes the cookies — the session cookie and the short-lived
  sign-in ones, and nothing else;
- it describes where Uploads and Generations live: your servers, your
  GPU machines, with the 24-hour and retention-day rules above;
- it describes visibility: only the user and, read-only, Admins; no
  public links; no IP addresses but an Admin's own;
- it describes the Audit log: kept permanently, naming the acting
  Admin with their address and browser; non-Admin users' entries are
  recorded without their name or email;
- **if your Studio is in approval mode**, it adds a section explaining
  what happens while an account is waiting — and that no email is sent
  while waiting;
- it ends with a *Questions?* line — your contact address if you've set
  one, otherwise a link to this project's GitHub repository — and a
  *Studio version:* line.

## Adding your own sections

The admin's **Site settings → Privacy** form (the same versioning
mechanics as the site terms, in [Site terms](site-terms.md)) lets you
set a **contact address** and add free-form sections of your own. The
generated section is always present and always last. Setting the contact
address also turns on the footer's *Contact · Report abuse* link, which
points at it.

## A pre-publish checklist

Before you open sign-ups to the public, walk this list:

1. **Set a contact address** in Site settings → Privacy. It is what the
   privacy page's *Questions?* line points at, and it turns on the
   footer's *Contact · Report abuse* link.
2. **Decide your retention.** Set `ASSET_RETENTION_DAYS` and
   `LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` in `.env` if you want automatic cleanup;
   otherwise Assets are kept until their owner deletes them.
3. **Confirm you still serve no third-party scripts.** The Studio
   serves none; if you've added your own (an analytics snippet, a chat
   widget), the generated page's "no third-party scripts" claims are
   now false — remove the scripts or rewrite the claims to match.
4. **Publish the privacy page before opening sign-ups.** It's linked
   from the footer and from the terms flow; a user should be able to
   read it before they hand over their identity.
